01 · THE BASICS

3 kinds of elections, no cap

every election you'll ever hear about fits into one of these three.

🏘️
local · panchayat / BBMP-GBA

local body elections

you're picking who fixes your street, garbage pickup, water supply. closest to your actual life.

🏛️
state · vidhan sabha · every 5 yrs

assembly elections

you elect an MLA. majority runs the state, picks the Chief Minister. Karnataka's current Assembly runs till 2028.

🇮🇳
national · lok sabha · every 5 yrs

general elections

you elect an MP. majority across the country forms the central government, picks the PM.

18 also means you can finally get your licence.

the whole DL process, top-rated driving schools near you, and how to actually pass the test without panicking.

01 · THE BASICS

the DL journey, no cap

it's a 3-stage thing.

📝
stage 1 · learner's licence (LL)

pass a rules quiz, get your LL

computer test on road signs + rules at the RTO. valid 6 months.

🚘
stage 2 · practice window

practice with your "L" plate

wait at least 30 days before the DL test, book within 180 days.

🏁
stage 3 · permanent DL

pass the driving test

practical test at the RTO track. pass it, get your card.

02 · STEP BY STEP

how to actually get it done

1

check eligibility

18+ cars/geared two-wheelers, 16+ with guardian consent for gearless <50cc, 20+ for commercial.

2

apply for your LL online

Form 2 on Sarathi, upload docs + photo, pay ~₹200–350.

sarathi.parivahan.gov.in
3

take the LL test

computer quiz on signs/rules, score 60%+ to pass.

4

get your LL, start practicing

display the "L" sign, practice with someone licensed.

5

book your DL test slot

after 30 days (before 180), Form 4, pay ~₹300 + ~₹200 fees.

6

take the driving test

H-pattern reverse, figure-8, gradient hold, sometimes parallel parking.

7

get your DL

permanent licence, valid 20 years for non-commercial, couriered to you.

05 · PASS IT FIRST TRY

how to actually clear the test

📋 carry on test day

  • original LL + photocopy
  • original ID/address proof
  • 2 passport photos
  • application/fee receipt
  • your own vehicle if required

🎯 what the test checks

  • H-pattern reverse
  • figure-8 / slalom
  • gradient hold, no rollback
  • parallel parking (some RTOs)
  • signal + indicator response
practice techniques that work: dry-run the figure-8 and H-reverse in an empty lot before test day. Practice clutch-brake balance on a slope. Say your mirror-checks out loud (SM-OM-SM). Run the sign-recognition mock tests on Parivahan's site the night before. Walk the actual test track beforehand if your RTO allows it.
