01 · THE BASICS

3 kinds of elections, no cap

they stack up from your street to the whole country.

🏘️
local · panchayat / BBMP-GBA

local body elections

you're picking who fixes your street, garbage pickup, water supply.

🏛️
state · vidhan sabha · every 5 yrs

assembly elections

you elect an MLA. majority runs the state, picks the CM. Karnataka's current Assembly runs till 2028.

🇮🇳
national · lok sabha · every 5 yrs

general elections

you elect an MP. majority across the country forms the central government + PM.
